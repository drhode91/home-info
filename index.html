Rhode Home Assistant Nest

This is a personal-use application connecting one private residence's Nest thermostat to a self-hosted Home Assistant instance for home automation and monitoring.

Privacy

This app only accesses data from the Nest devices in this one home. Data is used solely for local home automation and is never shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party.

Terms of Service

This app is for personal, non-commercial use only, operated solely by its owner for their own residence.
